 App Review Notes
==============================================================

Bayze AI is an AI chat app for iOS.

This resubmission uses build 10. Build 9 was uploaded successfully but was
rejected during App Store Connect binary processing because it was built with
the iOS 18.5 SDK. Build 10 is built with Xcode 26 / iOS 26 SDK.

What changed in build 10:

1. Sign in, account registration, and password reset are now handled inside the
   iOS app with native screens. The app no longer opens the default web browser
   for sign in or registration.

2. Because the app supports account creation, it now also supports in-app account
   deletion:
   Settings -> Account -> Delete account

3. Account creation remains optional. The app opens directly into chat as a
   guest, so users can use the built-in iOS chat experience without registering.

4. The iOS app contains no purchases, no subscriptions, no credit purchases, no
   premium feature purchases, no pricing page, and no external purchase links or
   calls to action.

How to verify:

- Launch the app.
- The app should open directly into chat as a guest.
- To test native sign in, go to Settings -> Account -> Sign in.
- To test native registration, go to Settings -> Account -> Create a free account.
- To test password reset, open the native Sign in screen and tap "Forgot password?"
- To test account deletion after signing in, go to Settings -> Account -> Delete account.

 Resolution Center Reply

Hello,

Thank you for your review and for the detailed feedback. We have addressed the
issues in the new build (1.0.0, build 10).

We understand that the previous review appears to have reviewed build 6. Build 7
and build 8 did not reach App Store Connect because they failed during the EAS
iOS build process. Build 9 was uploaded successfully, but App Store Connect later
flagged it for ITMS-90725 because it was built with the iOS 18.5 SDK. Build 10
is built with Xcode 26 / iOS 26 SDK.


== Guideline 4 (Design) — in-app sign in and registration ==

The app no longer sends users to the default web browser to sign in or register.
Sign in, account registration, and password reset are now handled natively inside
the iOS app:

- Sign in: native email/password screen in the app.
- Register: native "Create account" screen in the app.
- Forgot password: native screen in the app that requests a reset email.

In addition, because the app supports account creation, it now also provides
in-app account deletion:

Settings -> Account -> Delete account

This permanently deletes the user's account and associated data.

An account is still optional. The app opens directly into chat as a guest, and
users can use the built-in iOS chat experience without creating an account.


== Guideline 2.1(b) — business model / paid digital content ==

To clarify the business model: the iOS app itself contains no paid digital
content, no subscriptions, no credit purchases, no premium feature purchases,
no pricing page, and no external purchase links or calls to action.

Answers to your questions:

1. Who are the users that will use the paid content in the app?

None. The iOS app has no paid content.

2. Where can users purchase the content that can be accessed in the app?

Nowhere in the iOS app. The iOS app offers no purchases and shows no purchase
links or calls to action.

3. What specific types of previously purchased content can a user access in the app?

None. The iOS app does not unlock or access previously purchased content.

4. What paid content, subscriptions, or features are unlocked within the app
   that do not use In-App Purchase?

None. The iOS app provides only free built-in AI chat features. There are no paid
models, credits, subscriptions, premium features, purchase buttons, pricing
pages, or external purchase links in the iOS app.

5. Do users have to pay a fee to create an account?

No. Account creation is free, and an account is not required to use the app.
Guest mode lets users chat immediately without registration.

Please let us know if any further information would be helpful. Thank you for
your time.

Best regards,
The Bayze AI Team
