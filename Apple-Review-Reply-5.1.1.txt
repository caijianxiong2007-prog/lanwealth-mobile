------------------- 英文(贴进 Resolution Center 发送) -------------------

Hello,

Thank you for your review and for pointing out Guideline 5.1.1(v).

We have addressed the issue: the app no longer requires users to register or
log in to use the AI chat.

In the new build (1.0, build 6), which we have just submitted:

1. On launch, the app opens directly into the chat as a guest. An anonymous
   session is created automatically and no personal information is requested.
   Guests receive free trial credits, so the chat is fully functional without
   any account.

2. To verify, simply open the app and send a message in the chat — no login is
   required. (If a sign-in screen ever appears, tap "Continue as guest" to
   enter immediately.)

3. Registration and sign-in are now entirely optional. They are only offered
   for account-based features: syncing chat history across devices, using
   premium models, and topping up credits. Free models are available to guests;
   premium models are shown as locked and prompt sign-in.

If you prefer to review the signed-in experience, a test account is available:
  Email: apple-review@lanwealth.com
  Password: Bayze-Review-2026

Please let us know if any further information would be helpful. Thank you for
your time.

Best regards,
The Bayze AI Team
