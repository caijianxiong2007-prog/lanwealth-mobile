Hello,

Thank you for the detailed feedback. We have addressed both items in the new build (1.0, build 7).

== Guideline 4 (Design) — in-app sign in / registration ==

The app no longer sends users to the external web browser. Sign in, account
registration, and password reset are now all handled natively inside the app:

- Sign in: native email/password screen in the app.
- Register: native "Create account" screen in the app (no browser).
- Forgot password: native screen in the app that requests a reset email.

In addition, as account creation is supported, the app now offers in-app
account deletion: Settings → Account → "Delete account", which permanently
deletes the user's account and associated data.

(Note: an account is still optional — the app opens directly into the chat as a
guest. Sign in is only needed to sync chat history across devices.)

== Guideline 2.1(b) — business model / paid content ==

To clarify: the iOS app contains no paid digital content, no subscriptions, and
no purchases of any kind. It is a free AI chat app. Answers to your questions:

1. Who are the users that will use the paid content in the app?
   None. The iOS app has no paid content; all features in the app are free.

2. Where can users purchase the content that can be accessed in the app?
   Nowhere. The iOS app offers no purchases and shows no purchase links.

3. What specific types of previously purchased content can a user access in the app?
   None. The iOS app does not unlock or access any previously purchased content.

4. What paid content, subscriptions, or features are unlocked within the app
   that do not use In-App Purchase?
   None. The iOS app provides only free AI models. There are no paid models,
   credits, subscriptions, or premium features in the iOS app.

5. Do users have to pay a fee to create an account?
   No. Account creation is free, and an account is not required at all (guest
   mode lets users chat immediately).

If anything further would help, we're glad to provide it. Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
The Bayze AI Team
