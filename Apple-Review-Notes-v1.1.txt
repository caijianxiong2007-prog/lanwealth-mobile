------------------- 英文(贴进 Review Notes / Resolution Center) -------------------

Hello,

This is a feature update for Bayze AI. Two notes for your review:

1) New input options
The chat now supports attaching a photo from the library, taking a photo with
the camera, attaching a text/code file, and dictating a message by voice. These
use the standard camera, photo library, microphone, and speech-recognition
permissions, each with an in-context usage description. No content is uploaded
until the user sends a message.

2) Business model — no in-app purchases (Multiplatform, Guideline 3.1.3)
The app still contains NO purchases, NO prices, and NO links to any external
purchase. It sells nothing.

Users can use the app for free, including as a guest with no account (the app
opens directly into the chat). A free or guest user has access to the free
models only.

Separately, customers who have purchased a plan on our website can sign in and
access the additional models included in the plan they already bought — this is
access to a service they purchased on another platform (our website), consistent
with Guideline 3.1.3 (Multiplatform Services). The app never offers to sell
these plans, shows no pricing, and contains no purchase or "upgrade" links.

How to verify:
- Free/guest: launch the app and chat — only free models are listed.
- Purchased account: sign in with the test account below; the full model list is
  available because this account already holds a plan purchased on the web. No
  purchase UI appears anywhere in the app.

Test account (holds an active plan purchased on the web):
  Email: apple-review@lanwealth.com
  Password: Bayze-Review-2026

We're happy to provide any further detail about the multiplatform model. Thank
you for your review.

Best regards,
The Bayze AI Team
